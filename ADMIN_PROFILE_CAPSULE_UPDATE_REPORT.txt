6. ACCESSIBILITY IMPROVEMENTS
================================================================================

- Proper button semantics: Trigger is a native `<button type="button">`.
- ARIA Attributes:
  * `aria-expanded={isOpen}` reflects open/close state.
  * `aria-haspopup="menu"` indicates dropdown menu capability.
  * `aria-controls="admin-profile-dropdown"` associates trigger with menu panel.
  * Menu panel includes `role="menu"` and `aria-orientation="vertical"`.
  * Sign Out button includes `role="menuitem"`.
- Keyboard Navigation:
  * Dropdown opens on Enter or Space.
  * Pressing `Escape` closes the dropdown and returns focus to the capsule trigger.
- Click-Outside Dismissal:
  * Native `mousedown` document listener automatically dismisses dropdown on
    clicks outside the capsule container.
- Dropdown automatically closes upon clicking Sign Out.
